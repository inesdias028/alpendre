1Alpendre at a glance

Alpendre is a private platform for managing several holiday houses. Each house is a workspace with its own calendar, reservations and connections to Airbnb, Booking.com and its own website, so a night sold anywhere is blocked everywhere.

One calendar

Stays from every channel on one calendar per house, plus a timeline of all houses side by side. Double bookings are flagged in red.

Direct bookings

The house website shows live availability and sends booking requests straight into Alpendre, with no commission.

Team and email

Personal accounts by invite, each person sees only their own houses, and guests get a confirmation email in their language.

2How the pieces fit together

GitHub Pages can only host static pages, so Alpendre is split in two: the dashboard you look at (GitHub Pages) and the server that does the work (Cloudflare Workers).

PieceWhat it doesBuilt with
DashboardEverything you see and click: houses, calendar, bookings, connections, settings, people. It holds no data itself; it asks the server.Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (public/)
WorkerSigns people in, stores and checks reservations, reads the Airbnb/Booking/website calendars, publishes Alpendre's own calendar links, receives website bookings and sends emails.JavaScript on Cloudflare (worker/index.js)
Durable ObjectKeeps all the data (houses, reservations, people, sessions, activity log) in Cloudflare's storage. Every request goes through it one at a time, so data never gets mixed up.Cloudflare Durable Objects (SQLite)
SchedulerWakes the Worker every 5 minutes to sync the calendars, even when nobody has the app open.Cloudflare Cron Trigger
MailSends confirmation emails through Gmail with an app password, with the house logo embedded.worker/mail.js and a copy of the worker-mailer library
Booking widget / APILets any website show availability and take booking requests.public/embed.js and /api/public/…

3How it was made, step by step

Each step was built, tested on this computer first (with a local copy of the server and sample calendars), then published. The decisions below explain why it works the way it does.

13 Nov 2026 · step 1

Name, logo and first version

Named Alpendre (porch) with a logo of three porch arches under a tiled roof, one for each road a booking arrives by. Brand colours: ink green, cream, terracotta, olive and sun yellow, with the Fraunces and Inter typefaces. The first version managed one house, with a small guest booking page.

step 2

One workspace per house

Reworked into a platform where each house is its own workspace with its own Airbnb, Booking.com and website connections. Added the "All houses" home with house cards and a timeline. Why: you manage several houses, and your own website already existed, so the built-in guest page was replaced by a way to connect any website.

step 3

Live on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare

The server was rewritten to run on Cloudflare Workers with a Durable Object for the data, and the dashboard published on GitHub Pages. Why: GitHub Pages cannot run a server. Cloudflare's free plan is always on, never sleeps, and can run the sync on a schedule. Sign-in moved to tokens because the dashboard and the server live on different addresses. The owner password became a Cloudflare secret.

step 4

Personal accounts and invites

Each person has their own username and password. New people join through a single-use invite link and choose their own password, so nobody else ever sees it. The activity log records who did what. Tomás Martins Dias was invited this way.

step 5

Each person sees only their own houses

Houses belong to whoever creates them. The owner account sees all houses and can move a house to someone else. Managing people became owner-only. Why: otherwise anyone could reset another person's password and get into their houses. A flaw where two overlapping requests could act as the wrong person was found during testing and fixed.

step 6

Sync every 5 minutes

Alpendre checks the Airbnb, Booking.com and website calendars every 5 minutes. Airbnb and Booking.com read Alpendre's links on their own schedule (usually every 1–3 hours), which nobody outside can change. Truly live sync needs a certified channel manager.

step 7

The Quinta do Cabeço website connected

The website's own booking section (PT/EN, monthly prices, deposit) was kept and connected behind the scenes: its calendar reads Alpendre's availability and its form sends requests into Alpendre, with the price the guest saw.

step 8

Confirmation emails

Confirming a booking emails the guest in their language, sent through Gmail with an app password. The first account was blocked by a Google security check, so the sender became cabecoreservations@gmail.com.

step 9

iPhone app and phone layout

Alpendre can be added to the iPhone home screen as an app with its own icon. The phone layout got a top bar with a house switcher, bottom tabs, a + button, booking cards and a calendar with a list of stays, with every desktop feature within one or two taps.

13–16 Nov 2026 · step 10

Logo in the email

A per-house "Logo in emails" setting. The logo is embedded inside the email so it shows even when email apps block images. The dashboard's files were given version numbers so phones always load the newest copy.

4Using the app: every feature

All houses (home)

  • Summary numbers: number of houses, how booked the next 30 days are, arrivals in the next 7 days, website requests waiting, double bookings.
  • House cards: this month's occupancy, whether the house is occupied tonight, the next arrival, and which connections work (green), fail (red) or are missing (grey). The owner also sees who each house belongs to.
  • Timeline: every house side by side for 4 weeks, coloured by channel. Use the arrows to move.
  • Coming and going (next 7 days) and Needs attention (double bookings, requests to answer, failed syncs, unconnected channels).

Inside a house

SectionWhat you can do
OverviewOccupancy and revenue this month, arrivals, website requests, the next 14 days, alerts, and where bookings come from.
CalendarThe month with every stay coloured by channel: Airbnb Booking.com Website Manual. Click a stay to open it, or an empty day to add one. Striped bars are pending requests; red outlines are double bookings.
ReservationsSearch by guest, email or reference; filter by upcoming/past, channel and status.
ConnectionsAirbnb, Booking.com and website links (see sections 5 and 6), "Sync this house now", the activity log, and "Replace export links".
House settingsName, location, colour, check-in/out times, who it belongs to (owner only), website booking rules (price, cleaning fee, minimum nights, max guests, currency, auto-confirm), the confirmation email's extra message and logo, and deleting the house.

A reservation

  • Open any stay to edit the guest name, email, phone, guests, total, reference, notes and status (confirmed, pending, blocked, cancelled).
  • Stays imported from Airbnb/Booking.com keep the channel's dates (they update on every sync). Guest details are yours to add, and they are kept.
  • Website requests arrive as pending, with Confirm booking and Decline buttons. Confirming can email the guest (section 8).

Settings (owner)

People (invite, new link or password reset, remove), Calendar sync interval (5, 15, 30 minutes, 1 or 3 hours), Guest emails status, and Remove demo houses. Everyone else sees My account with "Change my password".

5Calendar sync with Airbnb and Booking.com

Neither platform offers an open API to small hosts. Both support calendar sync (iCal), and Alpendre uses it in both directions for each house.

1 · Import (their bookings into Alpendre)

Copy the export link from the platform and paste it into Alpendre (house → Connections).

Airbnb: Calendar → listing → Availability → Connect calendars → Export calendar.
Booking.com: Extranet → Rates & Availability → Sync calendars → Export.

2 · Export (Alpendre's bookings onto them)

Copy Alpendre's link for that channel and paste it into the platform's Import calendar, named "Alpendre". Each link lists the nights booked everywhere else, so the platform never sells them twice.
DirectionHow fast
Website → AlpendreInstant (the website writes straight into Alpendre)
Alpendre → websiteInstant (the website reads availability live)
Airbnb / Booking → AlpendreUp to 5 minutes (or press Sync now)
Alpendre → Airbnb / BookingUsually 1–3 hours, on their schedule

What the sync does, and doesn't

  • Calendar links carry dates and a reference only, never guest names or prices. Add those yourself; later syncs keep them.
  • "Not available" or "Closed" entries are imported as blocked dates.
  • A stay that disappears from a channel's calendar is marked cancelled.
  • Overlapping stays in a house are flagged as a double booking in red everywhere.
  • Alpendre ignores its own dates when a channel sends them back, and refuses to import its own link (which would loop).

The 1–3 hour gap

Two guests could book the same nights on different platforms before they refresh. Alpendre flags it immediately but cannot prevent it. To reduce the risk, avoid same-day bookings and use Sync now after a booking. Only a certified channel manager (for example Beds24 or Smoobu, about €10–30 per property per month) gives near-live sync.

If an export link leaks

House → Connections → "Export links leaked?" → Replace export links. The old links stop working; paste the new ones into Airbnb, Booking.com and the website.

6The Quinta do Cabeço website connection

The website kept its own design and bilingual booking section, and talks to Alpendre through its public API.

  • Its calendar shows Alpendre's availability (Airbnb, Booking.com and direct bookings together). If Alpendre cannot be reached, it falls back to the site's own files.
  • Its form sends each request into Alpendre as a pending website reservation, with the price shown to the guest (the site's monthly prices plus cleaning), adults and children, the deposit and method, the guest's language, and a reference like WEB-1A2B3C.
  • Two guests cannot request the same dates: the second is told they are taken.

Where it is set

In the website's script.js, the SITE.alpendre setting holds the server address and the house's public key:

alpendre: {
  api: 'https://alpendre.houseguests.workers.dev',
  key: 'pk_597b87235e444e064a75aa10',   // Quinta do Cabeço's public key
},

The public key comes from Alpendre → house → Connections → Your website → API. It is safe to show; it only allows reading availability and sending requests. Remove the key and the site works as before (requests by email).

Keep these in step

The Alpendre house settings must match the website's CONFIG: minimum 3 nights and up to 8 guests. Otherwise Alpendre refuses some requests the site allows.

Other ways to connect a website (for future houses)

OptionWhen to use it
Booking widgetA site with no booking system (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, plain HTML). Paste the two-line snippet from Connections; add data-mode="calendar" for availability only or data-accent="#hex" for your colours.

7People, access and security

AccountSigns in withSees and can do
OwnerUsername empty + the owner passwordEvery house (with owner names), move houses between people, invite/reset/remove people, sync interval, demo data
Invited people (e.g. Tomás, username tomas)Their username or email + their own passwordOnly their own houses (ones they add or the owner moves to them), with full control of those; change their own password

Inviting someone

  1. Settings → People → + Invite a person: full name (username fills in), optional email.
  2. Copy the link (or "Write an email with the link") and send it only to that person.
  3. They open it, choose a password (at least 8 characters) and are signed in. The link works once and expires after 7 days.
  4. Lost link or forgotten password: New link / Reset password next to their name. Removing someone signs them out at once and hands their houses to the owner.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored only as strong hashes (PBKDF2). Nobody can read them back, including the app.
  • Sign-ins last 30 days. Wrong passwords are slowed down.
  • Only the GitHub Pages dashboard (and this computer, for testing) may use the owner API from a browser.
  • Every request checks who is signed in and which houses they may touch. This was tested with 27 automatic checks, including overlapping requests.

8Confirmation emails

When you click Confirm booking, the guest receives an email from cabecoreservations@gmail.com in the language they used on the website (Portuguese or English).

What the email contains

  • The house logo at the top (embedded in the email)
  • Arrival and departure with check-in and check-out times
  • Nights, guests, total and the booking reference
  • The house's extra message (for example deposit instructions, address, who to call)
  • Replies go to the Gmail inbox

Controls

  • Untick "Email … a confirmation" before confirming to skip it
  • Send again on confirmed stays
  • If sending fails, the booking is still confirmed and the reason is shown
  • Settings → Guest emails shows whether sending is set up

Per-house settings

House settings → Confirmation email: Logo in emails (a https link to a PNG or JPG; Quinta do Cabeço uses …/quintadocabeco/images/brand/logo-email.png, a cropped copy on the cream background so it stays visible in dark mode) and Extra message.

The Gmail app password

  1. Signed in as cabecoreservations@gmail.com, turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Create an app password named "Alpendre" at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (16 letters).
  3. Save it with npx wrangler secret put GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD. Check there are 16 asterisks before pressing Enter.

Emails stop if…

…the Gmail account's password changes, 2-Step Verification is turned off, or the "Alpendre" app password is deleted. Create a new app password and save it again. If Google says it can't confirm it's you, try from your usual phone or computer on home Wi-Fi, or wait a day or two.

12Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
"Wrong username or password"Owner: leave the username empty. Others: ask the owner for Reset password.
A connection is redHouse → Connections shows the error. Check the link was copied completely; press Sync this house now.
Double booking alertOpen the stays and cancel one on its own channel (Airbnb/Booking/website).
"Gmail refused the login"The app password is wrong or was removed: create a new one and save it again (section 8).
Email without the logoThe house has no logo link saved: House settings → Logo in emails, then Save.
Email in SpamMark it "Not spam"; future emails go to the inbox.
The app looks old or a field is missingReload the page, or close and reopen the iPhone app.
Website says dates are available but Alpendre doesn'tWait a few seconds and reload: the site reads Alpendre live. Check the house's minimum nights and max guests match the site.
A request was refused on the websiteHouse → Connections → Your website → Booking widget: "Accept booking requests from my website" must be ticked. Also check minimum nights and max guests.

13Ideas for later

  • More emails: one to you when a website request arrives, a decline email to the guest, and reminders before arrival.
  • Online deposit payment (MB WAY, Multibanco, card), for example with easypay, already prepared in the website code.
  • Channel manager (Beds24 or Smoobu) for near-live Airbnb/Booking sync with names and prices; shared houses and view-only roles (for cleaners, for example).
